Open qadam-teacher.html in your browser and refresh after changes. The open reading book follows your scroll position and turns pages at chapters and subsections. Use its arrows to navigate, the expand button to enlarge it, or minus to minimize. On mobile it starts minimized. Click table headings to sort, click or keyboard-select rows to highlight, and scroll wide tables sideways. Calculator inputs remain synchronized. Photographs embedded in the standalone file are available offline; external photographs, readings and fonts require internet access. Editable modular sources are included in the ZIP. Hosting remains incomplete because repository network access was unavailable.
